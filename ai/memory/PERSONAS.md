# 👥 User Personas

Detailed definitions of the primary user roles to guide UI/UX and business logic decisions.

## 1. The Buyer (Người mua)
- **Identity**: Everyday consumers looking for products through social discovery and search.
- **Core Needs**:
  - Seamless discovery via Social Feed and Short Videos.
  - High trust through reviews, likes, and shop ratings.
  - Fast and secure checkout process.
  - Real-time interaction with sellers.
- **Key Features**: Product Search, Infinite Video Feed, Cart, Checkout, Order Tracking, Chat.

## 2. The Seller (Người bán)
- **Identity**: Individuals or businesses selling products and using content (posts/videos) for marketing.
- **Core Needs**:
  - Easy product and inventory management.
  - Tools to create "Video Commerce" content.
  - Analytics on sales, engagement, and video performance.
  - Efficient order fulfillment and communication tools.
- **Key Features**: Shop Dashboard, Product CRUD, Video Upload, Order Management, Sales Analytics.

## 3. The Affiliate (Người tiếp thị liên kết)
- **Identity**: Content creators and influencers who promote products to earn commissions.
- **Core Needs**:
  - Simple way to find high-commission products.
  - Easy link sharing and tracking system.
  - Transparent commission reporting and payout management.
- **Key Features**: Affiliate Dashboard, Product Selection, Tracking Link Generator, Payout History.

## 4. The Admin (Quản trị viên)
- **Identity**: Platform operators responsible for safety, moderation, and system health.
- **Core Needs**:
  - Tools to moderate content (posts, videos, comments).
  - User and Shop management (Ban/Unban, Verification).
  - Oversight of platform-wide transactions and disputes.
  - System performance and health monitoring.
- **Key Features**: Admin Panel, Content Moderation, Dispute Resolution, Platform Analytics.
